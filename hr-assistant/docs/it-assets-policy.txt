IT ASSETS POLICY

Document Owner: IT Department (in coordination with Human Resources)
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 1 October 2026
Review Cycle: Annually
Applies To: All employees, interns, contractors and consultants of the Company

======================================================================
1. PURPOSE
======================================================================

This policy explains how the Company issues, manages, protects and recovers
its IT assets. It sets out what employees can expect to receive, how they
must look after Company equipment, and what happens when equipment is lost,
damaged, upgraded or returned.

The goals of this policy are to:
- Give every employee the tools they need to do their job well.
- Protect Company and customer data stored on or accessed through devices.
- Keep a clear record of who holds which asset at all times.
- Control costs by extending the useful life of equipment.
- Comply with applicable data protection, software licensing and
  environmental regulations.

======================================================================
2. SCOPE
======================================================================

This policy applies to:
- Full-time and part-time employees.
- Interns and trainees.
- Contractors, consultants and temporary staff who are issued Company assets.

It covers all IT assets owned, leased or paid for by the Company, including:
- Laptops and desktop computers
- Mobile phones and tablets
- Monitors, docking stations, keyboards, mice and headsets
- Data cards, dongles and portable Wi-Fi devices
- External storage devices (USB drives, external hard disks)
- Software licences and SaaS subscriptions
- Access cards, security tokens and hardware authentication keys
- Peripherals such as printers, webcams and conference equipment

Personal devices used for work (Bring Your Own Device, "BYOD") are covered
in Section 9.

======================================================================
3. DEFINITIONS
======================================================================

IT Asset: Any hardware, software or digital resource owned, leased or
licensed by the Company.

Asset Holder: The employee to whom an asset is formally assigned.

Asset Tag: The unique identification label or number attached to each
hardware asset and recorded in the asset register.

Asset Register: The central inventory maintained by the IT Department that
records each asset, its specifications, holder, location and status.

IT Helpdesk: The IT support team, reachable through the IT ticketing portal,
email (itsupport@company.com) or the internal chat support channel.

Refresh Cycle: The standard period after which a device becomes eligible for
replacement.

======================================================================
4. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
======================================================================

4.1 Employees (Asset Holders)
- Use assets mainly for Company business and in line with this policy.
- Keep assets physically safe and in good working condition.
- Report loss, theft or damage to the IT Helpdesk within 24 hours.
- Do not remove asset tags, security software or management profiles.
- Return all assets in working condition on exit or when requested.

4.2 Managers
- Raise asset requests for new joiners at least 7 working days before
  the joining date.
- Approve requests for additional or higher-specification equipment.
- Make sure departing team members return all assets before their last
  working day.

4.3 IT Department
- Buy, configure, tag, issue and track all IT assets.
- Keep the asset register accurate and up to date.
- Install and maintain security controls (encryption, antivirus, device
  management).
- Provide repairs, replacements and technical support.
- Securely wipe and dispose of retired assets.

4.4 Human Resources
- Inform IT of new joiners, transfers and exits in a timely manner.
- Include asset return in the exit clearance process.
- Coordinate recovery of costs for lost or damaged assets where applicable.

4.5 Finance
- Approve budgets for asset purchases.
- Process cost recoveries and allowance reimbursements.
- Maintain depreciation records.

6. LAPTOPS AND DESKTOPS
======================================================================

6.1 Issuance
- Laptops are issued on the first working day after the employee signs the
  Asset Acknowledgement Form (digital or physical).
- Each laptop comes pre-installed with the operating system, standard
  productivity software, security tools and device management software.
- The employee must check the device and report any visible damage or
  missing items within 2 working days. After that, the device is taken to
  have been received in good condition.

6.2 Standard Configuration
Every Company laptop will have:
- Full-disk encryption (BitLocker for Windows, FileVault for macOS).
- Company-approved antivirus / endpoint protection.
- Mobile Device Management (MDM) enrolment.
- Automatic operating system and security updates.
- A screen lock that activates after 5 minutes of inactivity.
- Company VPN client.

6.3 Acceptable Use
Employees must:
- Lock the screen (Windows: Win + L, macOS: Ctrl + Cmd + Q) whenever they
  step away.
- Keep the operating system and applications updated; do not postpone
  updates for more than 3 days.
- Store work files on Company-approved cloud storage, not only on the local
  disk, so data is backed up.
- Connect to public Wi-Fi only through the Company VPN.

Employees must not:
- Disable or uninstall security software, encryption or MDM.
- Install unlicensed, pirated or unapproved software.
- Allow family members, friends or any unauthorised person to use the device.
- Use the device for illegal activities, personal commercial work, crypto
  mining or accessing offensive content.
- Open the device casing or attempt hardware repairs themselves.
- Change administrator settings or create unauthorised local accounts.

6.4 Limited Personal Use
Reasonable personal use (for example, checking personal email or reading
news during breaks) is allowed if it does not interfere with work, use
excessive bandwidth or break any Company policy. Employees should not store
large personal files (photos, videos, music) on Company devices. The Company
is not responsible for personal data stored on Company devices, and such data
may be deleted during repairs, reimaging or exit.

6.5 Refresh Cycle
Device Type                    | Refresh Cycle
-------------------------------|--------------
Standard laptop                | 4 years
High-performance laptop        | 3 years
Desktop computer               | 5 years
Monitor                        | 5 years

A device may be replaced earlier if it fails repeatedly, cannot run required
software, or is no longer supported with security updates. Upgrades outside
the refresh cycle require manager and IT approval.

6.6 Desktops
Desktops are used for specific roles such as lab, testing, design or
reception work. They stay at the assigned office location and must not be
moved without IT approval.

======================================================================
7. MOBILE PHONES AND TABLETS
======================================================================

7.1 Eligibility
Company mobile phones are provided to roles that need to be reachable outside
the office or work with customers in the field, mainly Sales, Customer
Success, Support on-call staff, Managers and Senior Leadership (see Section
5.1). Other employees may receive a phone where there is a clear business
need approved by the department head.

7.2 Mobile Plans
- The Company pays for a standard voice and data plan selected by IT and
  Finance.
- International roaming must be activated through the IT Helpdesk before
  business travel. Roaming charges for personal travel are paid by the
  employee.
- Charges for premium-rate services, in-app purchases, paid apps or excess
  personal usage above the plan will be recovered from the employee.

7.3 Security Requirements
- Devices must be enrolled in MDM before Company email or apps are installed.
- A PIN of at least 6 digits or biometric lock is mandatory.
- The device must not be jailbroken or rooted.
- Apps may only be installed from official app stores.
- Automatic updates must remain switched on.
- IT may remotely lock or wipe the device if it is lost, stolen or if the
  employee leaves the Company.

7.4 Phone Number Ownership
Company-provided phone numbers belong to the Company. On exit, the number is
retained by the Company unless HR and the department head approve a transfer
in writing.

7.5 Refresh Cycle
Company smartphones are replaced every 3 years, or earlier if the device is
no longer supported with security updates.

7.6 Mobile Phone Allowance (Alternative)
Employees who are eligible for a Company phone may instead choose to use
their personal phone and receive a monthly mobile allowance, as set by
Finance. In this case the BYOD rules in Section 9 apply.

======================================================================
8. ACCESSORIES, PERIPHERALS AND OTHER EQUIPMENT
======================================================================

8.1 Standard Accessories
Accessories such as chargers, mice, keyboards, headsets and laptop bags are
issued along with the main device and recorded against the employee's name.

8.2 Replacing Accessories
- Accessories that fail due to normal wear and tear are replaced free of
  charge after the faulty item is returned to IT.
- Accessories lost by the employee will be replaced once free of charge per
  year. Further replacements are charged at current cost.

8.3 External Storage Devices
- USB drives and external hard disks are blocked by default on Company
  laptops.
- Where there is a business need, IT will issue an encrypted Company USB
  device. Personal USB drives must not be used.
- Confidential or customer data must never be copied to removable storage
  without written approval from the data owner and IT Security.

8.4 Portable Wi-Fi Devices and Data Cards
These are issued to employees who travel frequently or work in the field.
Usage is monitored, and excessive personal use may be recovered from the
employee.

8.5 Shared and Office Equipment
Printers, conference room systems, projectors and shared monitors are Company
assets managed by IT and Facilities. Faults should be reported through the IT
ticketing portal. These items must not be removed from the office.

======================================================================
9. BRING YOUR OWN DEVICE (BYOD)
======================================================================

9.1 Permitted Use
Employees may access limited Company services (email, calendar, chat and
approved collaboration apps) from personal mobile phones and tablets.
Personal laptops may not be used to access Company systems unless approved
by IT Security for a specific business reason.

9.2 Requirements
To use a personal device, the employee must:
- Register the device with the IT Helpdesk.
- Install the Company's MDM or app-protection profile, which keeps Company
  data in a separate, managed work area.
- Use a supported and up-to-date operating system.
- Keep a screen lock (PIN, password or biometric) switched on.
- Not jailbreak or root the device.

9.3 Privacy
The Company manages only the work profile or Company apps on a personal
device. It does not access personal photos, messages, call history or
personal apps. If the device is lost or the employee leaves, IT will remove
only Company data and the work profile, unless a full wipe is required to
protect Company data and the employee has been informed.

9.4 Costs and Support
The employee is responsible for the purchase, repair and running costs of a
personal device, except where a mobile allowance is paid under Section 7.6.
IT will provide support only for Company apps and the work profile.

======================================================================
10. SOFTWARE AND LICENCES
======================================================================

10.1 Approved Software
Only software on the Company's approved software list may be installed. The
list is available on the IT intranet page. Standard software is installed by
IT at the time of issue.

10.2 Requesting New Software
Employees can request new software through the IT ticketing portal. Requests
are reviewed by IT Security for security, licensing and data privacy risks.
Typical review time is 3 to 5 working days.

10.3 Licence Compliance
- All software must be properly licensed. Using pirated or cracked software
  is strictly prohibited and may lead to disciplinary action.
- Company licences must not be installed on personal devices unless the
  licence allows it and IT approves.
- Licences are reclaimed when an employee changes role or leaves.

10.4 Cloud Services and AI Tools
Employees must not sign up for external cloud services, file-sharing sites or
AI tools using Company email, or upload Company or customer data to them,
unless the tool has been approved by IT Security. Approved AI tools are
listed on the IT intranet page.

======================================================================
11. SECURITY AND DATA PROTECTION
======================================================================

11.1 Passwords and Authentication
- Passwords must be at least 12 characters and must not be reused across
  systems.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, VPN and all
  critical systems.
- Passwords must never be shared, written down near the device, or sent over
  email or chat.
- The Company password manager should be used to store work passwords.

11.2 Physical Security
- Never leave devices unattended in public places, vehicles or hotel lobbies.
- Do not leave a laptop visible inside a parked car. If unavoidable, lock it
  in the boot out of sight.
- Carry laptops as hand luggage when flying; never check them in.
- Use a privacy screen when working in public spaces with sensitive data.
- Store devices in a locked drawer or take them home at the end of the day
  if working from a shared office.

11.3 Monitoring
Company devices, networks and accounts may be monitored for security,
compliance and troubleshooting purposes, in line with applicable law.
Employees should have no expectation of privacy for activity on Company
devices and systems, except as provided by law.

11.4 Reporting Security Incidents
Employees must report suspected phishing emails, malware, unusual device
behaviour or any possible data breach to the IT Helpdesk or
security@company.com immediately.

======================================================================
12. LOSS, THEFT AND DAMAGE
======================================================================

12.1 Reporting
If a Company asset is lost or stolen, the employee must:
1. Inform the IT Helpdesk within 24 hours (immediately, if possible) so the
   device can be locked or wiped remotely.
2. Inform their manager.
3. In case of theft, file a police report within 48 hours and share a copy
   with IT and HR.

12.2 Cost Recovery
Each case is reviewed by IT and HR. Cost recovery is decided as follows:

Situation                                         | Employee Liability
--------------------------------------------------|---------------------------
Normal wear and tear or manufacturing defect      | None
Theft with police report, reasonable care taken   | None (first incident)
Accidental damage (first incident in 12 months)   | None
Accidental damage (repeat within 12 months)       | Up to 50% of repair cost
Loss due to negligence (e.g. left unattended in   | Up to 100% of current
public place or unlocked car)                      | depreciated value
Deliberate damage or misuse                       | 100% of repair or
                                                  | replacement cost, plus
                                                  | possible disciplinary action
Failure to report loss within 24 hours            | May be treated as negligence

The depreciated value is calculated by Finance using straight-line
depreciation over the device's refresh cycle. Recovery may be made through
payroll deduction with the employee's consent, or through the final
settlement, as permitted by law.

12.3 Replacement
A replacement device is normally provided within 3 working days of reporting.
A loaner device may be provided in the meantime where available.

======================================================================
13. REPAIRS AND SUPPORT
======================================================================

- All repairs must be arranged through the IT Helpdesk. Employees must not
  take Company devices to outside repair shops.
- Before handing a device over for repair, employees should make sure their
  work files are synced to Company cloud storage.
- A loaner device will be provided if the repair is expected to take longer
  than 2 working days, subject to availability.
- Remote employees may be asked to courier devices to the nearest office.
  Courier costs are paid by the Company.

IT Helpdesk Service Levels:
Priority  | Example                                     | Response Time
----------|---------------------------------------------|---------------
Critical  | Lost/stolen device, security incident,      | 1 hour
          | cannot work at all                          |
High      | Device not booting, no network access       | 4 working hours
Medium    | Software issue, peripheral not working      | 1 working day
Low       | New software or accessory request           | 3 working days

======================================================================
14. REMOTE AND HYBRID WORK
======================================================================

- Employees working remotely use the same Company-issued laptop and must
  follow all security rules in this policy.
- Home Wi-Fi networks should use WPA2 or WPA3 encryption and a strong
  router password.
- Company devices must not be connected to shared or public computers, and
  Company data must not be printed on public printers.
- Eligible remote employees may claim a one-time home office setup allowance
  for items such as a monitor, chair or desk, as set by Finance. Items
  purchased under the allowance belong to the employee unless stated
  otherwise.
- Employees must have IT approval before taking Company devices to another
  country for work, as some countries have restrictions on encrypted
  devices and data transfer.

======================================================================
15. TRANSFERS AND ROLE CHANGES
======================================================================

When an employee changes role, team or location:
- Their manager must inform IT at least 5 working days in advance.
- Equipment that is not needed in the new role must be returned.
- Software licences and system access no longer needed will be removed.
- Additional equipment for the new role will be issued per Section 5.

======================================================================
16. RETURN OF ASSETS ON EXIT
======================================================================

16.1 Return Process
- All Company assets must be returned on or before the last working day.
- Remote employees will receive a prepaid courier kit and must ship assets
  within 3 working days of their last working day.
- IT will check each asset against the asset register and confirm its
  condition.
- The employee must not reset, wipe or reformat devices themselves. IT will
  perform a secure wipe after any required data has been backed up.

16.2 Personal Data
Employees should remove personal files and log out of personal accounts
(for example, personal iCloud, Google or browser accounts) before returning
devices. Activation locks such as "Find My iPhone" must be disabled.

16.3 Exit Clearance
IT clearance is part of the HR exit clearance checklist. The full and final
settlement will be processed only after IT confirms that all assets have been
returned. The cost of unreturned or damaged assets will be recovered under
Section 12.2.

16.4 Purchase of Old Devices
In some cases, the Company may offer an exiting employee or a current
employee the option to buy their old device at its depreciated value when it
reaches the end of its refresh cycle. All Company data will be securely wiped
before transfer. This is at the Company's discretion and is not an
entitlement.

======================================================================
17. ASSET DISPOSAL
======================================================================

- Retired devices are securely wiped using industry-standard data erasure
  methods, or physically destroyed if they cannot be wiped.
- A certificate of data destruction is kept for each disposed device.
- Disposal is done only through certified e-waste recyclers, in line with
  applicable environmental laws.
- Devices still in working order may be donated to approved charities after
  secure wiping, with Finance and IT approval.

======================================================================
18. ASSET AUDITS
======================================================================

- IT conducts an asset verification audit at least once a year.
- Employees must respond to asset verification requests within 5 working
  days, for example by confirming the asset tag or sharing a photo of the
  device.
- Assets that cannot be accounted for will be treated as lost and handled
  under Section 12.

======================================================================
19. POLICY VIOLATIONS
======================================================================

Breaking this policy may lead to:
- Removal of device access or privileges.
- Recovery of costs as described in Section 12.2.
- Disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment.
- Legal action where the law has been broken.

The level of action depends on the seriousness of the violation, whether it
was intentional, and whether it is a repeat offence.

20. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
======================================================================

Q: When will I get my laptop as a new joiner?
A: On your first working day, after you sign the Asset Acknowledgement Form,
   provided your manager raised the request at least 7 working days before
   you joined.

Q: Can I use my Company laptop for personal tasks?
A: Limited, reasonable personal use is allowed (see Section 6.4), but avoid
   storing personal files and never install unapproved software.

Q: Am I eligible for a Company mobile phone?
A: Company phones are provided to Sales, customer-facing staff, managers,
   senior leadership and on-call support roles. Others may receive one with
   department head approval for a business need.

Q: My laptop is slow and old. Can I get a new one?
A: Laptops are replaced every 3 to 4 years depending on type. If your device
   is failing or cannot run required software, raise a ticket with IT for an
   early replacement review.

Q: I lost my laptop charger. What should I do?
A: Raise a ticket with IT. One lost accessory per year is replaced free of
   charge; further replacements are charged at current cost.

Q: My laptop was stolen. Will I have to pay for it?
A: If you took reasonable care, reported it within 24 hours and filed a
   police report, you will not have to pay for the first incident. Losses
   due to negligence may be recovered (see Section 12.2).

Q: Can I install software myself?
A: Only software from the approved list. For anything else, raise a request
   through the IT ticketing portal for a security review.

Q: Can I use ChatGPT or other AI tools for work?
A: Only AI tools approved by IT Security may be used with Company data. Check
   the approved list on the IT intranet page.

Q: Can I access Company email on my personal phone?
A: Yes, after registering the device and installing the Company's MDM or
   app-protection profile (see Section 9).

Q: Can I take my Company laptop abroad?
A: For business travel or remote work from another country, get IT approval
   first. Always carry the laptop as hand luggage.

Q: What do I need to return when I leave?
A: Every asset assigned to you, including the laptop, charger, phone, SIM,
   monitors, accessories, access cards and security keys, by your last
   working day.

Q: Can I keep my laptop when I leave?
A: Not by default. At the Company's discretion, you may be offered the
   option to buy an end-of-life device at its depreciated value.

Q: Who do I contact for IT asset issues?
A: The IT Helpdesk via the IT ticketing portal, itsupport@company.com, or
   the internal IT support chat channel. For security incidents, also email
   security@company.com.
